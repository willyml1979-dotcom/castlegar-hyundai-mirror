2020 Hyundai Palisade Ultimate AWD – Luxury | 7 Passenger | Fully Loaded

The2020 Hyundai Palisade Ultimate AWDbrings together premium luxury, impressive space, advanced technology, and confident all-weather capability. As the top-tier Palisade trim, it offers an exceptional combination of comfort and versatility for families who want something truly upscale.

Powered by a3.8L V6 producing 291 horsepower and 262 lb-ft of torque, paired with an8-speed automatic transmission and HTRAC All-Wheel Drive, the Palisade delivers smooth, confident performance with plenty of power for highway driving, road trips, and everyday use.

TheUltimate trimis loaded with premium features includingNappa leather seating, heated and ventilated front seats, heated second-row seats, heated steering wheel, panoramic sunroof, premium Harman Kardon® audio, navigation, wireless charging, power-folding third-row seats,and ahands-free power liftgate.

The spacious7-passenger cabinfeatures second-row captain's chairs and a comfortable third row, giving passengers plenty of room while maintaining excellent cargo flexibility. Fold the rear seats down and you have substantial space for luggage, sports equipment, groceries, or weekend gear.

Advanced Hyundai SmartSense technology includesBlind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist, Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist, Lane Keeping Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Highway Driving Assist,and aSurround View Monitor.

The Palisade also features aBlind-Spot View Monitor, giving you a live view of the vehicle's blind spot when activating the turn signal—a standout feature that adds another level of confidence behind the wheel.

Luxury, space, AWD capability, and technology come together beautifully in the 2020 Hyundai Palisade Ultimate AWD.

Fully loaded, incredibly spacious, and built to keep the whole family comfortable—this Palisade Ultimate is ready for the road ahead.
