2014 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Rubicon – 4x4 | Off-Road Ready | Legendary Capability

The2014 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Rubiconis the ultimate combination of legendary Jeep capability, rugged styling, and four-door practicality. Built for drivers who want to go beyond the pavement, the Rubicon is engineered to tackle challenging terrain while remaining versatile enough for everyday driving.

Powered by a proven3.6L Pentastar V6 producing 285 horsepower and 260 lb-ft of torque, paired with a5-speed automatic transmission, the Wrangler delivers strong performance both on and off the road.

The legendaryRubicon 4x4 systemtakes capability to another level withRock-Trac 4WD, electronic locking front and rear differentials, a disconnecting front sway bar, and heavy-duty Dana 44 axles. Whether you're navigating snow, mud, rocks, or rugged trails, this Wrangler is built to take you there.

TheUnlimited four-door configurationprovides significantly more passenger and cargo space than the traditional two-door Wrangler, making it a much more practical choice for families, gear, and weekend adventures.

Inside, you'll find a durable and functional cabin with features such asheated front seats, touchscreen infotainment, Bluetooth connectivity, cruise control, power windows and locks,and removable/foldable rear seating for added versatility.

The removablehardtop and doorsgive you the freedom to enjoy open-air driving when the weather is right, making the Wrangler experience unlike anything else.

Legendary Rubicon capability, V6 power, four-door versatility, and true open-air freedom—the 2014 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Rubicon is built for adventure.
