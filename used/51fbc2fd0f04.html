2023 Ford Bronco Sport AWD – Adventure Ready | Turbocharged | Built for Every Journey

The 2023 Ford Bronco Sport AWD is the perfect SUV for drivers who want rugged capability, everyday practicality, and unmistakable Bronco styling. Whether you're commuting through the city or exploring off the beaten path, the Bronco Sport is engineered to handle it all with confidence.

Powered by a responsive 1.5L EcoBoost® turbocharged engine producing 181 horsepower and 190 lb-ft of torque, paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, the Bronco Sport delivers spirited performance while maintaining excellent fuel efficiency. Standard All-Wheel Drive provides exceptional traction and control, making it ready for every season.

Built with adventure in mind, the Bronco Sport features Ford's Terrain Management System™ with G.O.A.T. Modes™ (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain), allowing you to easily adapt to snow, sand, mud, and more for confidence wherever the road—or trail—takes you.

Inside, you'll find a spacious and functional cabin equipped with Apple CarPlay & Android Auto, a touchscreen infotainment system, Bluetooth connectivity, backup camera, push-button start, and versatile cargo space with 60/40 split-folding rear seats to accommodate all your gear.

Ford Co-Pilot360™ safety technology includes Blind Spot Information System, Lane Keeping Assist, Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, and Auto High-Beam Headlamps, providing peace of mind on every drive.

Rugged, capable, and packed with modern technology, the 2023 Ford Bronco Sport AWD is the ideal SUV for drivers who want everyday comfort with true adventure-ready capability.

Bold Bronco styling, turbocharged performance, and standard AWD—this Bronco Sport is ready for wherever your next adventure begins.
