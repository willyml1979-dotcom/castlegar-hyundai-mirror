2026 Hyundai Tucson Ultimate PHEV AWD – Plug-In Hybrid | Electric Range | Fully Loaded

The 2026 Hyundai Tucson Ultimate PHEV AWD combines the efficiency of electric driving with the freedom of a gasoline engine. With its bold styling, premium interior, advanced technology, and confident AWD capability, this Tucson is an excellent choice for drivers looking to reduce fuel costs without giving up versatility.

Powered by a 1.6L turbocharged plug-in hybrid powertrain producing approximately 268 horsepower, paired with a 6-speed automatic transmission and HTRAC All-Wheel Drive, the Tucson delivers smooth, responsive performance while offering the ability to drive on electric power for shorter daily trips.

With an electric driving range of approximately 53 km, you can handle many daily commutes and errands using electric power alone when fully charged. For longer journeys, the gasoline engine provides the flexibility to keep going without needing to stop and recharge.

The Ultimate trim is loaded with premium features including leather seating, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, heated steering wheel, panoramic sunroof, power driver's seat with memory, premium Bose® audio, navigation, wireless charging, dual 12.3-inch displays, and a hands-free power liftgate.

Stay connected with Apple CarPlay & Android Auto, advanced infotainment, multiple USB-C charging ports, and Hyundai's latest connected-car technology.

Hyundai SmartSense safety technology adds confidence with features including Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot View Monitor, Surround View Monitor, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Following Assist, Highway Driving Assist, and Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist.

Electric efficiency, long-distance flexibility, premium luxury, and AWD capability come together in the 2026 Hyundai Tucson Ultimate PHEV.

Drive electric when you can, go wherever you want, and enjoy the premium features you expect from a top-of-the-line Tucson.
